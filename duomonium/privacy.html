Privacy Policy

Duomonium · Effective November 11, 2026

Duomonium is made by Waleed Kamran ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Data I collect

None. The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (such as pumping mode, reeds, scale, key labels and look) are saved on your iPhone so they're there next time. They never leave your device, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

Device features

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge angle to work the bellows. This happens only on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. All sound is generated on the device. The app doesn't use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. If you download it from the App Store, Apple may collect information under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
