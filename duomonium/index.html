Duomonium

A harmonium for iPhone Duo: pump the bellows with the hinge, play the keys below.

Contact support

How to play

  • Hold your iPhone Duo unfolded in portrait, half-folded like a laptop.
  • Rock the top half gently to pump air into the bellows.
  • Press the keys on the bottom half to play. Slide the strip above the keys to change octave.

Common questions

The sound fades away.
The bellows are running out of air. Keep rocking the top half, or choose Light pumping in Settings.

Can I play without pumping?
Yes. Turn on Auto-bellows in Settings.

Does it work without a foldable?
Yes. On any iPhone, drag the top half of the screen up and down to pump.

How do I change the key?
In Settings, set Sa to any of the 12 notes. You can also switch between Sargam and Western labels.

No sound?
Check that your iPhone isn't in Silent mode and the volume is up.
