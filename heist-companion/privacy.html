Privacy Policy

Heist Companion · Effective November 23, 2026

Heist Companion is made by Waleed Kamran ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: I don't collect anything about you, but the free version shows ads from Google, and Google collects some data to show them.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked cheats, favorites, missions and checklist items), your gamer name and your settings are saved on your iPhone. They never leave your device unless you export a backup yourself, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

Guide updates and notifications

The app downloads guide updates from my server. These requests contain no personal information. If you allow notifications for new cheat codes, your iPhone's push token (a random identifier from Apple) is sent to my server so it can notify you. It isn't linked to you, and turning alerts off in More removes it.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), an approximate location based on your IP address, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission. The app asks for permission to track only so ads can be more relevant. If you decline, you still get ads, just less personalized ones. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

EU, UK and Switzerland. You're asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice in the app under More → Ads → Privacy choices.

Remove Ads purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase that turns off all ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; I never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether you own Remove Ads and keeps that yes/no answer on your device. With Remove Ads, the app doesn't load Google's ad SDK at all.

Photos

If you tap Save image on a milestone card, the app adds that image to your photo library. It can't read your existing photos.

Children

The app is a guide for an adult-rated game and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
