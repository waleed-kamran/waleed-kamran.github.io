Common questions

How do I remove ads?
Go to More → Ads → Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?
Go to More → Ads → Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My purchase says it's waiting for approval.
If Ask to Buy is on, ads disappear as soon as the family organizer approves it.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes, progress is stored only on your iPhone. Use More → Export progress first, then Import progress afterwards.
