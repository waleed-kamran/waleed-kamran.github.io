Privacy Policy

PakAi · Effective November 24, 2026

PakAi is made by Waleed Kamran ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: I don't collect any personal information and there is no account, but the free version shows ads from Google, and Google collects some data to show them.

Car ads you check

When you share or paste car ad links and tap Check, your iPhone downloads those public ad and inspection report pages directly from the website they're on. They are read on your iPhone and are not sent to me. The app never asks for a login, cookies or passwords.

Paint-gauge photos in an inspection report are read with on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your iPhone). No photo is uploaded anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The links you entered, saved copies of the pages you checked, your language choice and the checklist items you ticked are kept on your iPhone so results open quickly and offline. They're deleted when you delete the app.

Market data

The app downloads an updated market price file from my website. These requests contain no personal information.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), an approximate location based on your IP address, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Tracking permission. The app asks for permission to track only so ads can be more relevant. If you decline, you still get ads, just less personalized ones. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

EU, UK and Switzerland. You're asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice with the Ad privacy link at the bottom of the home screen.

Remove Ads purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time in-app purchase that turns off all ads. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; I never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether you own Remove Ads and keeps that yes/no answer on your device. With Remove Ads, the app doesn't start Google's ad SDK.

Children

The app is a tool for people buying a car and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
