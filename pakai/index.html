PakAi

Share up to four used-car ads and PakAi reads each ad and its inspection report, checks the paint-gauge photos and compares the cars in English or Roman Urdu.

Need help?

Email wk.official98@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and, if possible, the ad link that didn't work. I usually reply within a few days.

Contact support

Common questions

How do I remove ads?
Tap Remove ads at the bottom of the home screen. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?
Tap Restore purchase at the bottom of the home screen, using the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A car says it couldn't be checked.
Check your internet connection and tap Check again. If the ad was removed or sold, it can no longer be read.

Does PakAi send my photos anywhere?
No. Paint-gauge photos from the inspection report are read on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
