Privacy Policy

Air Hockey Duo · Effective November 11, 2026

Air Hockey Duo is made by Waleed Kamran ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

Data I collect

None. The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only time it goes online is to check or make an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store.

Data stored on your device

Your game settings (such as goals to win, match time, theme, sound and haptics) are saved on your iPhone so they're there next time. They never leave your device, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

In-app purchase

Air Hockey Duo Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; I never see your name, Apple Account, card or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked, and keeps that yes/no answer on your device so Pro is ready when you open the app.

Device features

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position to pause the game and switch screens. This happens only on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. The app doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. If you download it from the App Store, Apple may collect information under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
